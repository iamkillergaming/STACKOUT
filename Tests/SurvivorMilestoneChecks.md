`SurvivorMilestoneChecks.cs` exercises the saved top-50 and top-10 announcements through real opponent admissions, normal READY/countdown/GO transitions, and `BotPlayer.Die` eliminations. Copy it into an isolated project's `Assets` after `OriginalSpriteSetup.BatchSetup`, then run Unity with `-batchmode -force-glcore -projectPath <isolated-project> -executeMethod SurvivorMilestoneChecks.Run -logFile <log>`. Omit `-quit` and `-nographics`: the fixture enters Play mode, captures the banners, and exits itself. Success prints `SURVIVOR_MILESTONE_CHECKS_PASSED`; failures exit with code 1. Only the isolated copy receives `Assets/SurvivorMilestoneChecks.unity`.

The saved-art checks require the AJN Aokane font, a centered board-width label above gameplay, the supplied `me_game_remain_1.wav` and `me_game_remain_2.wav` clips, and a dedicated non-looping 2D voice source. A full 99-player round stays quiet through matchmaking, READY, GO, 51 survivors, and 11 survivors. Reaching 50 and 10 while alive starts the corresponding English banner and original voice. The text's entrance scale must change across real updates, repeated standings observations cannot restart it, and the top-50 animation must end naturally. Player death cancels the active top-10 banner and voice; further spectator eliminations remain quiet.

A second full round crosses both thresholds in one frame and must present 50 followed by 10 without losing the queued announcement. Victory cancels the active banner and voice. Both rounds return through the normal retry path, proving thresholds reset for the next match. A final three-player round verifies that a small initial roster does not announce thresholds it never crossed, including after disabling and re-enabling the component. Player occupancy, queued pieces, board transforms, camera framing, and saved presentation/source identities remain unchanged by announcements.

The fixture pauses gameplay, shortens countdown/death-sweep timings, and mutes sources only in the isolated runtime. It uses the original milestone clips and checks actual `AudioSource.isPlaying`; it does not establish speaker output or subjective mix balance. Captures are written to `/tmp/tetris-top50-announcement.png` and `/tmp/tetris-top10-announcement.png`.
