`FullMatchSoak.cs` runs a complete natural bot match in an isolated copy of the final Unity project. Copy it into that copy's `Assets` directory and run Unity with `-batchmode -force-glcore -projectPath <isolated-project> -executeMethod FullMatchSoak.Run -logFile <log>`. Omit `-quit`; the fixture enters Play mode and exits itself. Keep graphics enabled because the real player initializes its sprite artwork through the rendering pipeline.

The source scene must contain MatchFlow and 98 saved inactive opponents. The fixture saves only `Assets/FullMatchSoak.unity`. It preserves and asserts every bot's authored 1.1-second interval both before admission and at GO. It applies the short READY/countdown presentation and slow player gravity at runtime and guards the manual admission clock; it does not rely on reflection edits before scene saving to persist prefab-instance overrides. A configuration log confirms the settings. The complete roster joins through `BattleArena.AdmitNextBot`. The fixture waits for actual GO, forces the player's sole elimination, then sets `Time.timeScale` to sixteen. This accelerates scaled gameplay together while retaining the relationship between the authored bot cadence, garbage countdowns, and clear delays. Bot intervals and garbage/clear settings are not overridden. There are no forced bot placements, bot eliminations, garbage packets, bag contents, target mappings, or board states: all 98 opponents use their real Update, reachable SRS planner, seven-bag, targeting, garbage cancellation/rise, and KO attribution until one remains. Random streams remain unseeded.

The run is limited to 300 wall-clock seconds after GO and 100,000 placements, with a separate 15-second startup limit. Every ten seconds it logs elapsed time, placements, accepted attacks/lines, natural bot eliminations, survivors, phase, the player's frozen rank, and maximum pending lines/plans per frame. A timeout or failure prints the same state and exits one instead of hanging.

LateUpdate observations group placements by each bot's actual `lastPlannedFrame`, including any GO-frame placements, and independently inspect the scheduler's current frame counter. This avoids treating two frames' newly observed placements as one frame. The global three-search budget and twelve-line pending cap must hold throughout. Success requires real accepted attacks and attributed KOs, exactly 97 natural bot elimination events plus the player's event, player rank 99 throughout spectating, all 98 bots retaining unique ranks 1–98, exactly one living winner, all bots paused with empty garbage queues, and MatchFlow's saved final standings UI visible. No board objects may be replaced or generated. Success logs `FULL_MATCH_SOAK_PASSED` with final metrics and exits zero; time scale is restored on completion, failure, or disable.

This is a variable-duration integration soak, not a deterministic strategy or frame-time benchmark. Its hard planning-count limit is checked directly; wall-clock performance and the exact winning bot can vary across machines and random matches.

Latest validation: Unity 6000.3.8f1 passed with 21,495 placements, 2,953 accepted attacks / 5,212 lines, 97 natural bot eliminations, 95 attributed K.O.s, and one winner. All 99 ranks were unique and the spectator retained rank 99. The uniformly accelerated run finished in 48.14 wall seconds with maximum pending garbage 12 and maximum frame plans 3.
